## Selected Dataset

The Yelp Dataset was selected as the initial public dataset for the WaSaa
service recommendation prototype.

The dataset contains business information and user reviews, including
business names, categories, geographical information, ratings, review
counts
and textual reviews.

The dataset is not Kenyan and therefore is not treated as representative
of Kenyan service providers. It is used as an external public dataset for
developing and evaluating the NLP service discovery component.

Relevant business categories are mapped to the five WaSaa service
categories:

- Healthcare
- Transport
- Beauty & Wellness
- Domestic Services
- Education Support

Only records that can be mapped to one of these categories are included
in the WaSaa prototype dataset.

The original source and licensing conditions will be retained in the
project documentation.
